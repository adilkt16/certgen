FAQ

Common questions about the certificate generator

A quick overview of how the bulk certificate generator works and what file formats it supports.

What is CertGen?

CertGen is a bulk certificate generator that helps you turn one template and a spreadsheet into many polished certificates with minimal manual work.

Can I use CSV or XLSX files?

Yes. Upload CSV or XLSX data, select the recipient name column, and generate certificates directly from the spreadsheet.

Is this a certificate generator from spreadsheet data?

Exactly. CertGen is built for certificate generator from spreadsheet workflows so you can go from names to finished certificates in one pass.

What can I download?

You can export individual certificates as JPEG or PDF, or bundle the full run into a ZIP for easy distribution.

Who is it for?

It is a good fit for schools, events, HR teams, clubs, and organizations that need fast CSV to certificates production.

Do I need to redesign each certificate?

No. You place the text once, then reuse the template for every row in your spreadsheet.
