Security

Updated: May 19, 2026

Our Approach

Security is a core priority. We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect data and maintain the integrity of the CertGen service. For more about data handling and user responsibilities, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Data Protection

  • Encryption: Data in transit is protected using TLS. Stored data is encrypted at rest where supported by our hosting provider.
  • Access control: Administrative access is limited and audited. We follow least-privilege principles.
  • Backups: We maintain regular backups to enable recovery from incidents.

Development & Operations

We perform code reviews, dependabot-style dependency monitoring, and apply security updates regularly. Production systems are monitored for anomalies.

Vulnerability Disclosure

If you discover a security issue, please contact us at adilkt16@gmail.com. Provide steps to reproduce and any supporting details. We will acknowledge and respond promptly.

Limitations

No system is entirely risk-free. We strive to reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to follow best practices for sensitive data and access control on their side, and to review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the full picture.
